Our CSR policy

1. Reduce our clients' environmental impact

Our ambition is to help clients reduce the environmental impact of their digital services. . We work on this every day to offset our own impacts. Over 100 million Europeans have indirectly benefited from our services.

2. Raise awareness of digital impact

Reducing digital impact requires awareness among all industry stakeholders. We regularly speak at schools, meetups and conferences for all profiles: CIOs, POs, designers, webmasters, CSR consultants…

Our shared position with DRI Group companies gives us a wider audience and greater impact. Alliances with Ademe, ADN Ouest, French Telecom Federation, EVEA, I-CARE, Norsys, Constellation and other responsible digital players strengthen visibility. We also raise awareness through our blog and working groups such as Green Code Lab, Green Lab Center and the Green Compagnons network.

3. Identify and share digital frugality best practices

Measuring our clients' applications and websites leads us to identify and recommend best practices. We contribute to GR491, RGESN, 115 web ecodesign best practices and Designers Éthiques frameworks. Our recommendations are backed by concrete measurement feedback. We are also expanding our scope with RGAA support.

4. Expand access to our services for associations

We offer Greenspector Studio at half price to non-profit associations and certain scientific projects (e.g. VU Amsterdam). This is one of the goals of our Mobile Efficiency Index tool.

5. Committed and transparent governance

  • Financial and remuneration transparency
  • Majority-based collegial decisions
  • Remote work for everyone, including interns
  • Autonomy: shared calendars, goal tracking, no time monitoring
  • Half-day birthday leave offered
  • Soft mobility encouraged — partnership with Transway to fund employees' low-carbon mobility
