France

Bronze partners — France

Kapela

Kapela creates, modernizes and maintains high-performance, sustainable business applications. Convinced that digital technology must reconcile innovation, efficiency and responsibility, Kapela integrates eco-design principles from the outset and concretely measures environmental impact through its partnership with Greenspector.

With the rise of artificial intelligence, Kapela is developing an approach focused on useful, controlled and responsible AI, serving both business performance and digital sobriety. Our ambition: to be one of the French pioneers of responsible AI applied to digital services.

Infogreen Factory

Infogreen Factory is a consulting and training company specialising in responsible digital technology. Made up of committed entrepreneurs, IF helps organisations with digital service ecodesign, accessibility and information system resilience by providing audits, strategies and implementation plans.

International

Bronze partners — other countries

Envite Consulting

Envite Consulting is a consultancy specializing in sustainable IT solutions. We help companies optimize resource efficiency in software development to minimize their environmental impact.

Through expertise in business process automation, software architecture optimization and software development, Envite Consulting encourages technological innovation with respect for the environment, creating future-ready software.

Technology

Technology partners

Aguaro — IT carbon footprint

Aguaro is a SaaS platform that gives IT and ESG leaders the means to drive down the environmental costs of their activity and influence employees, customers or suppliers.

By leveraging field teams and existing systems, organizations secure their goals and reduce risks while saving money and becoming more resilient for the future.

DRI — responsible, sovereign hosting

For over 20 years, DRI has offered expertise as a responsible, sovereign French web host and managed services provider. It is also a company built around shared passions such as open source, environmental footprint and new technologies.

Setelia — device testing expertise

Setelia is an IT services company specializing in Telecom, IT and Digital. Setelia supports Telecom clients as a testing laboratory.

The partnership with Greenspector expands Setelia's portfolio with a turnkey technical benchmarking offer for all types of connected devices or mobile applications.
