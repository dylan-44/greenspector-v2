Certificate

Le Certificate Greenspector de Sobriété de Service Numérique

The certificate is awarded to mobile applications and websites that combine energy frugality, data frugality and performance.

  • Energy frugality on user device: to reduce quality risks, improve inclusion and reduce impacts.
  • Data consumption frugality: to reduce environmental footprint and facilitate mobile usage.
  • Performance: to limit footprint while improving UX.

Measurements are grouped, analysed then converted to scores. An ecoscore from 0 to 100 summarises the whole, with three certificate levels: Bronze (50-69), Silver (70-89), Gold (90-100).

A badge displayable on your site or in your application is linked to an authentication page hosted by us.
