Diagnostics

Identify a battery or performance issue

Your business app is slow, or drains the battery too fast. Users complain. How do you find the root cause?

The precision and stability of measurements with Greenspector Studio enable our partners to help. They run measurement campaigns on devices from your own fleet.

By varying test conditions — with or without MDM, different models, network connections, apps — they pinpoint the cause and recommend remediation actions.

This approach can also be preventive: for example, you can assess the impact of an Android version upgrade before rolling it out.

Quality Gate

Validate business apps before you deploy them

A single bug in a single business app can drain your devices' batteries and bring your teams to a halt!

Stop reacting after the fact: before each app deployment, validate battery consumption and performance.

It is straightforward:

  • an automated test of the critical journey in each app;
  • triggered automatically by your CI/CD or by your testers;
  • on your private test bench, with your own fleet devices, for tailored results.

Your employees do not live in a simulation. With Greenspector Studio, neither do your tests!
