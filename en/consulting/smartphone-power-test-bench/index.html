Studies

R&D and studies… measure easily on real devices

Greenspector Studio is a strong fit for studies and R&D. Rigorous, precise and easy to deploy, it brings measurement of many metrics on real devices into your projects.

Example

For its R&D, a European mobile operator wants to compare the energy impact of different 5G video streaming formats. It builds an end-to-end test infrastructure: servers, antennas, devices… It gets precise metrics on its servers and network antennas.

But what about the devices? With Greenspector Studio, it can run a full measurement campaign comparing AVC, VVC and HEVC; CMAF and multicast; different video player settings, and more.

Hardware modules

R&D and studies… measure almost anything

We have integrated optional hardware measurement modules into Greenspector Studio. These modules sit between the object under test, your PC and Greenspector Studio, so you can collect and analyse any energy-consumption measurement in the platform.

That is how you can, for example, measure the energy consumption of a frost sensor before deploying it along railway tracks!

Depending on the object under test, full or partial test automation is possible.

Our study for the French Telecom Federation with iCare, involving Smart TV measurements, is another example of Greenspector Studio's capabilities.
