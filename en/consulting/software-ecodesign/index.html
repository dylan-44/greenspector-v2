Certificate

The Greenspector Digital Service Efficiency Certificate

The certificate is awarded to mobile applications and websites that successfully combine energy efficiency, data efficiency and user performance.

  • Energy efficiency on user device: inclusion, quality and impact reduction.
  • Data consumption efficiency: easier mobile usage and reduced impact.
  • Performance: lower footprint and better UX.

Measurements are analysed then converted to scores and an ecoscore from 0 to 100. The certificate is awarded at Bronze (50-69), Silver (70-89) or Gold (90-100) level.

A badge usable internally and externally is linked to an authentication page hosted by us.
