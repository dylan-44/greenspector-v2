How it works

Measure, understand, improve

1

Describe user journeys

Describe your user journeys in a few clicks, or use our simple, efficient DSL scripting language to test an Android app, an iOS app or a web journey.

2

Choose test conditions

Recent or older device, WiFi or cellular network : choose on our real device cloud the conditions suited to your goals. Then launch the test or let your CI/CD run it.

3

Manage with clear visibility

Battery consumption, performance, ecoscore, environmental impacts… View your results in the web interface or use our APIs to export key indicators to your dashboards. Identify improvement areas and optimise your app !
