FAQ

Have a question?

If you cannot find the answer below, contact us. Contact us

Can I try for free?

Yes, of course! Click "Try for free" and follow the steps. We are also available to give you a demo.

Do I need a credit card for the free trial?

No, you do not need to enter your credit card during the trial period.

What is the commitment period?

You subscribe for one month. The subscription renews the following month unless you cancel. You will receive a reminder email 3 days before each renewal date.

Do you offer annual subscriptions?

For now you can only subscribe to monthly plans from this site. However, if you are interested in an annual subscription, contact us and we will set it up for you with a 20% discount on the monthly rate.

What is a "Journey"?

With Greenspector Studio, you track sobriety, performance and environmental impacts at User Journey level. A Journey consists of an unlimited number of steps: click, page load, text input… For clarity, you can group steps into functional domains.

Can I add extra Journeys?

Yes, you can subscribe to additional User Journeys. Contact us!

How is measurement time counted?

New in 2026: measurement time is unlimited for Pro, Team and Enterprise plans 🙂
