Case study

French Digital Health Agency: measuring the CO₂ impact of health applications

The ANS set up a portal to measure and project CO₂ impact for web and mobile health applications, required for listing on Mon Espace Santé.

Request a demoDiscover Greenspector Studio

Context

A dedicated portal to assess the impact of health applications

The French Digital Health Agency wanted a dedicated portal to measure and project the CO₂ impact of web and mobile health applications.

This assessment is mandatory to be listed on Mon Espace Santé. The scheme covers two categories of applications:

The project had to allow publishers to perform their own assessment, with no prior training, while ensuring measurement reliability.

  • general applications;
  • teleconsultation applications.

Results

70+ publishers assessed on the Ecoscore Portal

  • Public evaluation and ranking of publishers by the French Digital Health Agency on the Ecoscore Portal.
  • 70+ publishers assessed.
  • Assessment performed by publishers themselves, with no prior training, thanks to Greenspector Studio's intuitive interface.

Keys to success

What made the difference

Self-service onboarding

The interface was reworked with a dedicated tutorial so publishers can complete their assessment independently.

Measurements on real devices

Measurements on real devices convinced the ANS of the reliability of the Greenspector approach.

A dedicated white-label portal

A dedicated white-label portal was deployed to display results and make them easy to consult.

Tests adapted to teleconsultation

Teleconsultation tests run on two devices simultaneously to reflect real usage conditions.
