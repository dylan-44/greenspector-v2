Case study

Région Bretagne: steering the ecodesign of a service portal

As part of its service portal redesign, Région Bretagne integrated a strong requirement: achieving maximum ecodesign level.

Request a demoDiscover Greenspector Studio

Context

A redesigned service portal with maximum ecodesign level

Région Bretagne launched a service portal redesign project to offer a new version of its digital services.

Among the strong requirements applied to this new version was achieving maximum ecodesign level.

The goal was to embed digital sobriety in design, development and maintenance practices for the portal, with regular tracking of results.

Results

Ecodesign driven by measurement, from build to maintenance

  • Ecodesign driven by results throughout the project, in build then maintenance phase, thanks to Greenspector Studio measurements.
  • Implementation of search with optimised semi-automatic input, reducing data volume by a factor of 5.
  • Publication of an ecodesign declaration presenting the results achieved.

Keys to success

What made the difference

Training project stakeholders in ecodesign

Project teams were trained upfront to share a common ecodesign culture and its challenges.

Defining frugality requirements from the start

Frugality requirements were defined upfront to guide functional, technical and editorial choices.

Adapting best practices to the portal context

Ecodesign best practices were adapted to the project's technical and functional context to remain concrete and actionable for teams.

Orienting the user experience towards frugality

A design review helped build a user experience consistent with digital sobriety objectives.

Measuring at each sprint

Greenspector Studio measurements were performed at each sprint during the build phase, with improvement recommendations based on observed results.
