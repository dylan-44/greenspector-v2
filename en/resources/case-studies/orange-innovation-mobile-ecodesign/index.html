Case study

Orange Innovation: supporting application ecodesign

Orange Innovation chose Greenspector to support application ecodesign and contribute to the Group's decarbonisation approach.

Request a demoDiscover Greenspector Studio

Context

A pilot to structure application ecodesign within the Group

Orange Innovation wanted to choose a tool to support application ecodesign in order to contribute to decarbonisation.

The pilot was led by the « Responsible Digital » programme and an Orange Poland team from CEC Software Quality.

The experiment was run on two very different applications:

  • MyHey, a Belgian mCare application;
  • Orange TV, a video-on-demand and streaming application.

Results

An internal service to support Group entities

  • Experts are convinced by the approach and results achieved.
  • Orange Innovation is setting up an internal service to support Group entities on application ecodesign.
  • Greenspector analyses are used as concrete support to raise awareness among management and teams.

Keys to success

What made the difference

Collaborative work between centres of expertise

The project benefited from the contribution of Corporate Centres of Expertise « Software Ecodesign » and « Software Quality ».

A pilot on two very different applications

The pilot was run on two applications with very different technical and business contexts, to verify the relevance of the approach in several situations.

A team already experienced with test tools

The user team already had strong experience with test and performance tools, which facilitated adoption of the approach.

Concrete analyses for awareness-raising

Greenspector results serve as awareness-raising support for management and teams, especially in CX Reviews.
