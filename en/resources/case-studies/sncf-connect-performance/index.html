Case study

SNCF Connect & Tech: reducing CO₂ impact on the booking journey

As part of the OUI.sncf app redesign, SNCF Connect & Tech integrated strong requirements for performance, digital sobriety and software ecodesign.

Request a demoDiscover Greenspector Studio

Context

Redesigning OUI.sncf with digital sobriety as a requirement

SNCF Connect & Tech led the redesign of the OUI.sncf app, used by millions of people to book train tickets.

The project had significant business stakes, with a clear ambition for sobriety and ecodesign from the design phase.

The goal was to improve the user experience while reducing the environmental impact of application journeys, especially on the TER booking journey.

Results

−19% CO₂ and a journey 8 seconds faster

  • 19% less CO₂ on the TER booking journey.
  • A user journey 8 seconds faster.
  • 300 KB fewer requests per booking, approximately 9 TB per year.
  • Two applications certified Silver level, for Android and iOS.

Keys to success

What made the difference

An audit before go-live

The application was audited before go-live to identify the main improvement levers.

Concrete improvement areas

Several optimisation paths were identified, especially on geolocation and data loading.

Implementation by the project team

Recommendations were taken on board by the project team, then followed up with a new measurement to verify the gains achieved.

An approach integrated throughout the project

Performance control and digital sobriety were integrated into requirements from the design phase and throughout the project.
