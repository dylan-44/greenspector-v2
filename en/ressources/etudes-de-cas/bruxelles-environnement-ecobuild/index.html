Case study

Mission context

As part of its digital transformation, Bruxelles Environnement wanted to make its showcase website more sustainable, integrating digital sobriety from the redesign phase.

The project follows a lifecycle logic (manufacturing, use, end of life) and the regional Responsible Digital dynamic led with Paradigm.

The ecodesign approach was formalised from the specification: methodology, objectives, best practice annexes and hosting-related criteria.

Client: Bruxelles Environnement
Completion date: 23/12/2022

  • Ecodesign integrated into methodology and project approach.
  • Digital sobriety objectives included in redesign requirements.
  • Best practice annexes and recommended audit tools.
  • Requirements for a hosting solution consistent with the environmental strategy.

Implementation

How frugality was maintained over time

After mockup audit, close support (2 days per 3-week sprint) helped prioritise key actions and guide the most frugal technical choices.

An initial benchmark on 30 pages of the old site served as reference. Equivalent pages on the new site were then measured daily to drive corrections and verify objectives were met.

The project shows that success depends on anticipation, clear governance of roles and continuous tracking involving all stakeholders.

Operational advice

Key takeaways

  • Integrate ecodesign from ideation, not only at development.
  • Train teams and clarify responsibilities before kick-off.
  • Objectify results with an environmental budget and regular measurements.
  • Accept schedule trade-offs to guarantee sustainable deliverable quality.

Feedback highlights that expert support accelerates team learning while securing the project trajectory.
