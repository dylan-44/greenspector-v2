Measure, compare, improve: the complete solution for controlled battery consumption and your AI impact

Are you developing an application that embeds AI and want to guarantee optimal battery life on all your users' smartphones?

Our unique test bench in Europe, combined with Greenspector Studio software, gives you the ideal solution to accurately assess your application's energy consumption, whatever the model (Android or iOS) and its year of manufacture.

Benefits

Why choose our solution?

A unique test bench in Europe

Our specialised infrastructure enables rigorous tests across a wide range of devices, covering all categories (entry-level, mid-range, high-end) and operating systems (Android & iOS).

Device booking and test parallelisation

With Greenspector Studio, you can easily book smartphones, automate your tests and run analyses in parallel to save time. No more waiting days for results: our tools speed up your testing and optimisation process.

Precise battery discharge analysis

Our solution lets you accurately measure the energy impact of your AI application on each smartphone category. You can then anticipate discharge time and adjust your fixes accordingly.
