Real performance

Measure application performance on real devices

Greenspector Studio assesses the performance of your user journeys on physical smartphones and tablets from different generations.

Choose the device and network according to your goals. Analyse results at journey and step level to easily identify pain points that affect your users.

Measurement precision

Ultra-precise battery consumption measurement

Our solution directly measures battery consumption throughout the user journey with fine granularity: µAh/s every 200 ms.

Direct, ultra-precise measurement provides a new perspective on the real behaviour of your mobile application, where classic profiling tools remain approximate.

Diagnostics

Identify and locate overconsumption

Greenspector Studio quickly identifies performance bugs, energy bugs and resource bugs in your applications.

System metrics

Energy consumption, inbound/outbound data, CPU, memory and Android metrics for fine-grained software behaviour analysis.

Targeted reports

Locate technical and design issues and prioritise fixes with detailed step-by-step reports.

Measurement strategy

Manage performance by results

Define your measurement strategy by identifying the main usage types of the application:

  • User with latest-generation phone and 4G
  • Mobile user with mid-range phone
  • User in low-coverage area with refurbished phone

Then define what is measured: pages or representative journeys covering main features, based on real usage statistics.

Continuous tracking

Integrate performance into your release cycle

Run a measurement before each release to detect anomalies as early as possible and track trends over time. Greenspector APIs let you trigger tests from your CI/CD pipelines and export results to your dashboards.
