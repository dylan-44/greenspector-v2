Performance investigation

Battery consumption issue? Measure, understand, fix

With Greenspector Studio, investigation is easy. Run reliable measurements, identify problematic features and fix them.

Use your favourite profiling tool wisely: it is powerful but time-consuming. With Greenspector Studio, you know what to look for.

At each release, keep hardware requirements (battery, CPU, RAM, data…) under control to keep your devices longer and save money.

Battery measurement

Battery consumption, precisely for each feature

Greenspector Studio measures battery consumption throughout your user journey, as you defined it, with hyper-precise measurement (in µAh/s every 200 ms!). You easily identify overconsumption hotspots.

Field conditions

SaaS test conditions, like in the field

Select the device model and network connection that match your needs.

On a consumer device or one from your own business fleet, with or without your MDM/EMM installed, on Wi-Fi or cellular… Get a diagnosis as close as possible to field conditions.

And because it is a SaaS solution, you can trigger all tests from your CI/CD (learn more).

Automation

Automate the test you want

GDSL automation capabilities let you assess software in different situations: changing settings (brightness, enabling interfaces…), choosing devices, selecting the network…

Easily run varied automated tests to analyse the conditions in which defects occur.

Test reports

Analyse test reports

An error or crash? Access test reports, review screenshots and understand what happened.

Hardware control

Keep hardware pressure under control

Why risk an energy bug in production? Validate before each release and keep an eye on long-term trends.

By controlling hardware pressure, you keep your devices longer and achieve substantial savings for your budget and the environment.
