Lifecycle

DevGreenOps

DevGreenOps is a natural evolution of the DevOps methodology. All 8 phases of the loop can be impacted by DevGreenOps.

Throughout the cycle, the goal is to adopt a frugality approach (of needs, resource consumption…) and control the environmental quality of the software product.

Greenspector Studio is mainly used for pre-release testing (2). It is also very useful to monitor production (3). And of course, run occasional measurements to verify the frugality of a technical or functional choice (1).

To learn more we recommend our article « How to move from DevOps to DevGreenOps » or this webinar (in French).
