Ecoscore

The Greenspector ecoscore: simple in appearance, robust in its calculation

Each journey step is assessed on three axes: display time, energy consumption and data consumption. For each axis, steps are categorised then converted to a score from 0 to 100.

  1. Each step is ranked by performance category.
  2. The volume of steps per category is used to calculate a score.
  3. Three scores are produced: energy, data, display.
  4. The final ecoscore is the average of the three axes, weighted between critical and non-critical steps.

The final ecoscore combines critical (70%) and non-critical (30%) steps to better reflect real usage.
