New KPIs

Environmental indicators and a truly representative ecoscore

The Greenspector ecoscore is based on a complete analysis of the user journey, taking into account the relative weight of measured steps and whether they are critical. It provides a reliable reading of your digital product's frugality level.

With Greenspector Studio, also obtain a rigorous assessment of carbon impact and other environmental indicators directly usable by your teams.

4-step journey

A simple workflow to manage frugality and performance

1

Prepare tests

Describe a user journey with TestBuilder and our GDSL language. The editor lets you quickly reproduce Android, iOS and Web journeys with simple commands, screenshots and ready-to-use templates.

A single tool lets you prepare tests, run them and compare results.

2

Run measurements on the right device

Select a device from the test bench that matches your goals: a recent model for current measurements, an older model to verify real journey accessibility, or a specific network quality to test robustness.

For business applications, you can also add devices from your mobile fleet as an option.

3

Analyse step by step

Review the dashboard to spot steps that degrade frugality or performance: energy overconsumption, response time, network dependencies, third-party services or technical anomalies.

Functional groupings and different reading levels make work easier for the whole team.

4

Improve continuously

Run a measurement before each release to detect anomalies as early as possible and track indicator trends. APIs let you trigger tests from your CI/CD pipelines and export results to your dashboards.
