User journey efficiency and impact: Measure, Understand, Improve

Prepare and run User Journey measurements on devices on our mobile test bench. Choose the device and network according to your goals.

Analyse results at journey level (CO2 impact, ecoscore) and at each step of the journey.

Identify overconsumption hotspots, performance issues, and improve journey efficiency!

New KPIs for your product: environmental impacts and ecoscore

Simple in appearance, the Greenspector ecoscore is based on a complete analysis of all user journey steps, across 3 different axes, taking into account whether measured steps are critical or not. The Greenspector ecoscore truly represents your journey's efficiency level.

With Greenspector Studio, obtain a rigorous, precise assessment of carbon impact and other environmental indicators. Learn more about impacts (methodology, indicators…)

1

Prepare tests with a simple, powerful editor

Describing a user journey is simple and fast with TestBuilder, our exclusive editor built on our proprietary GDSL language. It uses simple keywords (click, wait…) yet is powerful enough to faithfully reproduce the most complex user journeys.

Build your journeys step by step using commands available from integrated screenshots.

Use a single tool for Android, iOS and Web journeys.

2

Run measurements on a test bench device

Select the device model that matches your testing goals.

A mid-range device for routine measurements, or an older model to check whether the journey works for less privileged users? Or test on 3G to assess network dependency?

For business applications, ask us to add a device from your mobile fleet to the test bench (optional).

3

Analyse: which journey steps need improvement?

Visualise steps affecting performance and efficiency and identify the causes.

Review the dashboard to quickly identify problematic journey points: energy overconsumption, performance issues, request or third-party service problems… The interface offers several reading levels, from the simplest to the most detailed, to help your whole team understand.

Group steps by functional domain for easier reading.

You know which steps to improve to gain efficiency, boost performance and reduce impacts. You can prioritise your efforts effectively.

4

Improve continuously!

Run a measurement before each release to avoid anomalies and track indicator trends.

Use APIs to trigger tests from your CI/CD (GitLab CI, Azure CI, Jenkins…). Use other APIs to export results to your own dashboards.
