Project KPIs

New indicators to drive improvement

Deceptively simple, the Greenspector ecoscore is based on a complete analysis of all user journey steps on three axes, taking into account whether measured steps are critical or not.

The ecoscore is representative of the real frugality level of your journey and comes with detailed environmental indicators, such as carbon impact.
